## 📖 About VisionX Season 2

**VisionX Season 2** is a six-week online technology quiz competition organized by **Microsoft Student Club, PRPCEM**.

Following the journey of **VisionX Season 1**, Season 2 brings a new and more competitive experience where participants can test their technical knowledge, compete on weekly leaderboards, learn from detailed solutions, and maintain a six-week streak.

The competition is open to **students and technology enthusiasts** who want to challenge themselves, expand their knowledge, and compete with fellow developers and tech enthusiasts.

---

## ⚡ From Season 1 to Season 2

VisionX started with a simple goal — **make learning competitive, engaging, and rewarding.**

Season 1 created the foundation for this journey by bringing together participants who were ready to test their technical knowledge and challenge themselves.

**Season 2 takes that journey forward.**

With six consecutive weekly quizzes, a structured leaderboard, detailed solutions, and a streak-based championship, VisionX Season 2 is designed to encourage **consistency, learning, and continuous improvement.**

> **Season 1 was the beginning. Season 2 takes the challenge forward. 🚀**

# 🎯 Rules & Regulations

## 1. Quiz Schedule & Availability

1. The quiz will open every **Sunday at 12:00 AM IST**.
2. The quiz will close on the same Sunday at **11:59 PM IST**.
3. Participants must complete the quiz within this **24-hour window**.
4. Each weekly quiz will be available for one day only.

---

## 2. Account & Registration

1. Participants must create an account on the official VisionX quiz platform before attempting the quiz.
2. **OTP verification** is required during registration.
3. The OTP will be sent to the participant's registered email address.
4. Participants must ensure that they have access to the email address used during registration.
5. If the OTP is not received, participants should check their **Spam/Junk folder**.

---

## 3. Quiz Format & Structure

1. Each weekly quiz will consist of **20 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)**.
2. The total quiz duration will be **5 minutes**.
3. Participants must complete the quiz within the allotted time.
4. Once the 5-minute timer expires, the quiz will be **automatically submitted**, even if some questions remain unanswered.
5. Participants are responsible for ensuring a stable internet connection before starting the quiz.

---

## 4. 🎯 Attempts & Scoring

Each participant is allowed **one attempt per weekly quiz**.

| Result             | Score |
| ------------------ | ----: |
| ✅ Correct Answer   |    +1 |
| ❌ Incorrect Answer |     0 |
| ⏭️ Unanswered      |     0 |

Once an attempt is submitted, it **cannot be restarted or replaced**.

---

## 5. 🏆 Leaderboard & Ranking

1. A weekly leaderboard will be published within **48 hours** after the quiz closes.

2. Ranking will be determined using the following priority:

   **1. Higher total score**
   **2. Lower total completion time**

3. If both score and completion time are identical, a special **tie-breaker quiz** may be conducted.

4. Participants selected for a tie-breaker must participate within the specified time window.

5. Absence or non-attempt of the tie-breaker may result in forfeiture of the tied position.

---

## 6. 🔥 Six-Week Streak & Grand Finale

1. VisionX Season 2 consists of **6 weekly quizzes**.
2. Participants who attempt all **6 weekly quizzes consecutively** will maintain an **unbroken streak**.
3. Missing any weekly quiz will break the participant's streak.
4. Only participants who complete all **6 weekly quizzes consecutively** will be eligible for the **VisionX Season 2 Grand Finale Champion** title.
5. Participation in individual weekly quizzes does not automatically guarantee Grand Finale eligibility.

---

## 7. 🎁 Rewards & Recognition

1. The **Top 3 performers** will be recognized through the official MSC-PRPCEM channels.
2. Participants who attempt the quiz will be eligible for a **Certificate of Participation**, subject to successful registration and compliance with the event rules.
3. Physical swags and prizes will be distributed according to the eligibility criteria announced by the organizers.
4. Physical swags and prizes are **non-transferable**.
5. Physical rewards will only be distributed to eligible **PRPCEM students**, where applicable.

---

## 8. 📚 Quiz Solutions & Explanations

1. Official answers and detailed explanations will be published on the **official MSC-PRPCEM GitHub repository** within **48 hours** after the respective quiz closes.
2. Participants are encouraged to review the solutions to strengthen their understanding and improve their performance in subsequent quizzes.
3. Sharing unreleased questions, answers, or official solutions for the purpose of gaining an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.

---

## 9. ⚠️ Fair Play & Disqualification

Participants are expected to attempt every quiz fairly and independently.

The following activities may result in disqualification:

* Creating or using multiple accounts.
* Manipulating or exploiting the quiz platform.
* Attempting to bypass platform restrictions.
* Using unfair methods to gain an advantage.
* Sharing unreleased questions or answers.
* Any activity that violates the integrity of the competition.

The organizers reserve the right to verify participant activity and eligibility.

---

## 10. 📢 Organizer's Rights & Disclaimers

1. The organizers reserve the right to modify the quiz schedule, structure, rewards, or rules when necessary.
2. Decisions regarding **disqualification, rankings, tie-breakers, eligibility, and disputes** will be final and binding.
3. Certificates will be issued only to officially registered participants who meet the participation requirements.
4. In case of a major technical failure, server crash, platform outage, or other unavoidable circumstances, the quiz may be rescheduled at the organizers' discretion.
5. Any important changes will be communicated through the official MSC-PRPCEM communication channels.
6. The organizers reserve the right to cancel a participant's eligibility if any event rules are violated.
